15: 2. Country About
16: Overview: Afghanistan is a landlocked country bordered by Pakistan, Iran, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China. Kabul is the national capital and largest urban centre.
17: Geography: The country has highly varied terrain, including the Hindu Kush, high plateaus, valleys, deserts and fertile river basins. Major river systems include the Amu Darya, Kabul River, Helmand River and Hari River.
18: Economy: The economy remains heavily dependent on agriculture, trade, services, construction, mining and remittances. Economic activity has shown modest growth, but living standards remain under substantial pressure.
19: Government: Afghanistan is currently governed de facto by the Taliban / Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan, which has exercised control since August 2021. International recognition remains contested.
